The Model's Inputs

Three signals, one behavioral fingerprint

Every prediction is built from three measurable habits pulled straight from transaction history.

R

Recency

How recently did the customer purchase?

measured in days since last order
F

Frequency

How often does the customer purchase?

measured in number of orders
M

Monetary

How much does the customer spend?

measured in total spend

The Model's Outputs

Three segments, three strategies

Each customer is assigned to exactly one cluster by the K-Means model, mapped to a named business segment.

Cluster 0

Inactive / Lost

Long gone quiet — low recency, thin order history, minimal spend.

Action: Reactivation campaigns & win-back offers

Top tier
Cluster 1

High-Value / Loyal

Buys often, spends generously, and purchased recently — the core of retained revenue.

Action: Loyalty rewards, exclusive deals & premium benefits

Cluster 2

Regular / Low-Value

Steady but modest — moderate frequency with lower average spend.

Action: Cross-selling & targeted promotions
